# Context & Vocabulary Switching

When to use: Set the correct language register before drafting. Getting register wrong is immediately obvious to an experienced reviewer.

---

## Register by Funder Type
| Funder | Register | Tone | Emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Federal government | Formal, policy-aligned, technical | Objective, measured | Accountability, reach, value for money |
| Provincial government | Formal, program-aligned | Objective, service-focused | Service delivery, regional equity |
| Community foundation | Mission-driven, narrative | Warm, relational | Community impact, lived experience, partnership |
| Corporate giving | Business-friendly, outcome-focused | Professional, partnership | Brand alignment, employee engagement, impact per dollar |

## Register by Org Vertical
- **Healthcare/Social Services:** evidence-based, trauma-informed, wraparound services, health equity. High evidence standard.
- **Arts & Culture:** community access, cultural equity, artistic excellence. Moderate evidence standard.
- **Education & Youth:** strength-based language (not deficit), SEL, academic achievement. High evidence standard for outcomes.
- **Environment:** science-based targets, ecosystem services, biodiversity, measurable outcomes. Scientific data required.
- **Housing/Homelessness:** Housing First, harm reduction, "people experiencing homelessness" (not "homeless people"), by-name list. CMHC data.
- **Indigenous Organizations:** self-determination, cultural reclamation, community-led design. Use Nation/community names specifically. UNDRIP where relevant. Community-defined evidence carries weight.

## Vocabulary Matching Rule
Use the funder's exact vocabulary from the RFP. If the RFP says "marginalized populations," use that phrase — do not substitute synonyms.

**When in doubt: mirror the RFP's language back into the application.**

## Pre-Draft Switching Checklist
- [ ] Funder type identified
- [ ] Org vertical identified
- [ ] RFP vocabulary list extracted (terms used ≥2 times)
- [ ] Register set for this application
- [ ] Outdated or inappropriate terms identified for this vertical
