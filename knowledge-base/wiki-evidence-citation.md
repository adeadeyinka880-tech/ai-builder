# Evidence & Citation Standards

When to use: Choose the right evidence type for each claim and cite it correctly. Evidence strength signals credibility.

---

## Evidence Tier Hierarchy

**Tier 1 — Strongest**
- Peer-reviewed academic research (author, year, journal)
- Government statistics: Statistics Canada, Census Bureau, federal/provincial ministry data, CIHI
- Sector-specific databases: CMHC housing data, Crime Severity Index, school board data

**Tier 2 — Strong**
- Org's own outcome data: program evaluations, annual reports, intake/exit surveys (must be current)
- Community needs assessments (by org, municipality, or recognized research body)
- Government program evaluations of similar publicly funded programs

**Tier 3 — Supportive**
- Sector reports: United Way, Community Foundations of Canada, sector associations
- Local media/community reports (illustrating conditions, not for statistical claims)
- Client testimonials (human context and lived experience, not a substitute for quantitative evidence)

## Match Evidence to Claim Type
| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Problem is real and significant | Tier 1 — government stats, academic research |
| Population is underserved | Census, needs assessments, local government data |
| Approach works | Academic research, government program evaluations, org outcome data |
| Org has done this before | Org's own data, past grant reports |
| Community wants this | Needs assessment, testimonials, consultation records |
| X number of people affected | Statistics Canada/CIHI/Census — cite year and geography |

## Citation Format
- Academic/government: Agency or Author (Year). *Title*. URL if available.
- Org data: "According to [Org]'s 2023–24 Annual Report..."
- Testimonials: first name only or "a program participant" — never full names without consent
- All statistics must include year collected and geographic scope

## Hard Rules
- Never fabricate a statistic → `[HUMAN: insert statistic — suggested source: Statistics Canada / CIHI / municipal report]`
- Never cite a source that cannot be verified — no invented journal titles or URLs
- Never present Tier 3 as Tier 1
- Flag any stat older than 5 years as potentially outdated

## Localization Rule
National/provincial stats must connect to local context. If only national data: "While national data shows X, local conditions in [region] suggest..." and flag for human to add local evidence.
